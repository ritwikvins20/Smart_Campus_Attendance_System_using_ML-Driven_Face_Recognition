02 / INTRODUCTION

01 · WHAT IT IS

The camera is the register

Every student's face is their ID card — no paper registers, no calling out sixty names, no queue at a card reader.

02 · HOW IT WORKS

Face → 512 numbers → match

The webcam captures a face. The model compresses it into a unique 512-number embedding and matches it against the enrolled class in one comparison.

Time, trust, records

Roll call burns lecture minutes, invites proxies and leaves unsearchable paper trails. Class time should be spent teaching — not accounting.

Scan in. Mark in. Sync live.

Students walk past the laptop camera one by one. Each face is recognised and marked present instantly — and every mark syncs live to the smart board, where the faculty watches the class list fill in and can override any entry with one tap.
